# Authors and attribution

Yao Xu ([@cadamcat](https://github.com/cadamcat)) is the author and maintainer of this formalization project.

The mathematics is due to others: Li and Zhuang reduced Erdős Problem 824 to a supply of primes with smooth successors and adapted the weighted-dilation-graph argument of OpenAI's paper to supply them; the reduction follows Pollack and Pomerance. This repository formalizes their argument and does not claim a new mathematical result. The conditional part ports Li and Zhuang's own Lean code under its MIT license; see [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).

The author planned and reviewed the formalization. Lean checks the proof terms and their dependencies.
